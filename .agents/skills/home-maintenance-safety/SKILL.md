# Skill: Home Maintenance Safety and Accuracy

## Safety classification
- LOW: basic organizing, nonhazardous inspection, changing reminders, plain-language definitions. Can be drafted automatically but still needs source and quality checks.
- MEDIUM: ladders, cleaning chemicals, power tools, water systems, mold, seasonal protection. Include conditions, PPE/limitations and when to hire a qualified pro; require careful review.
- HIGH: mains electricity, gas, combustion appliances, structural failures, roofing, asbestos/lead disturbance, serious mold, life-safety equipment, hazardous repairs. Do NOT issue detailed DIY instructions without qualified verification; prefer recognition, shutoff/emergency guidance when appropriate and contacting licensed professionals/local authorities.

## Claims
- Verify frequency and intervals with manufacturer guidance when available; home age, system type, climate, use patterns and warranty requirements differ.
- Do not assert universal codes, permits, tax rules, insurance coverage or legally required inspections. Reference local authority and professional advice where relevant.
- Do not transform approximate cost or maintenance frequency into a guaranteed saving, lifespan or safety outcome.
- Protect readers from chemical mixing risks and dangerous invasive procedures. Avoid confident model-specific instructions without the actual manual.
- Clearly separate optional recommendations from safety-critical obligations.
- For calculators: show units, constraints, formulas, assumptions, ranges and edge-case handling; test representative inputs.

## Pre-publication checklist
[ ] Appropriate risk level recorded
[ ] Primary sources retained and links verified
[ ] Safety boundaries visible near steps
[ ] Scope/region/equipment dependencies stated
[ ] Numerical claims and tool outputs tested
[ ] No fabricated expert review, credentials or firsthand testing
[ ] Human signoff for medium/high-risk material when warranted

Useful sources: https://www.cpsc.gov/ , https://www.epa.gov/ , https://www.ready.gov/ and original equipment manuals.
