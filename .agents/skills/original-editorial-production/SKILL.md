# Skill: Original Editorial Production

## Non-negotiable editorial standards
- Write to solve the reader's task, not to hit an arbitrary word count. Use clean original structure and original explanation, worked examples and tested takeaways.
- Originality is **additional information or utility**, not simply changing synonyms in an existing article. Search and compare existing pages, then state Upkeep Atlas's unique contribution.
- Preserve factual accuracy with official manufacturer documentation, DOE/ENERGY STAR, EPA, CPSC, FEMA, government extension services and local authorities. Source critical numbers and timeframes inline. Verify links at publication.
- For data and claims, save a claim -> source -> access date -> page section ledger.
- Never invent field testing, home inspections, real-life experiences, survey data, a credentialed reviewer, photographed repairs, endorsements or an author identity. Identify the publisher as Upkeep Atlas Editorial Team only if true; disclose relevant AI assistance and human review status.
- Safety: low-risk observation/organization may be published after research and tests. Medium/high-risk hands-on repair involving ladders/roof/gas/electrical/fire/contaminated water/mold/lead/asbestos requires qualified human sign-off for procedural guidance. When absent, reframe as recognition, planning and professional referral, or mark NEEDS_REVIEW.
- A genuine correction/update changes dateModified, not cosmetic rewording. datePublished only when first published.

## Guide content anatomy
Reader question -> direct useful answer (2–4 concise sentences) -> applicability and limitations -> material/system identification -> practical steps and original asset -> decision criteria / when to call a pro -> dated source links -> related site tool -> revision/publisher note.
Use H2/H3s for meaningful concepts; do not create padding, fake testimonials, keyword-stuffed FAQs or generic conclusory filler.
An article should include a genuinely helpful original artifact where relevant: annotated photo, printable checklist, comparison table, tested example, own calculator or new decision flow. If none adds value, high-quality plain text can suffice.

## Publication contract
- Every important claim checked against a current source and recorded.
- Distinct canonical intent from all existing pages.
- Accurate dates, publisher and disclosure; safe language; edits retained in GitHub.
- Supporting media licensed and provenance recorded; internal links contextual.
- No placeholder pages, broken anchors, empty image areas or copied article paragraphs.
- Quality audit against at least 3 live result pages: specifically note added usefulness.
- Allow returning BLOCKED/NEEDS_REVIEW rather than claiming a draft is expert-approved.

## AdSense
Keep primary content substantial, readable and not obstructed by ads. Do not fake a review or add ads to compensate for a thin page. Check ad-policy changes before launch.
