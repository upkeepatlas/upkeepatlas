# Skill: Visual Design

## Outcome
Create a refined, modern, practical homeowner resource that feels authored by a thoughtful design team rather than generated from a dashboard template.

## Art direction
- Positioning: an independent, reassuring field guide for the house; useful, quiet, enduring.
- Palette starting point (adjust with contrast tests): warm paper #F7F6F2, pine #234238, ink #202824, mist #DFE7DE, soft clay #B27F62. Avoid generic blue/purple gradients.
- Typography: an elegant, restrained editorial serif for large headings; a highly legible humanist sans for interface and body. Prefer accessible system fonts until optimized and licensed webfonts can be self-hosted.
- Editorial grid: intentional asymmetry, generous margins, strong typographic hierarchy, short line lengths, meaningful whitespace, understated dividers, generous tap targets.
- Icon system: simple one-stroke or flat geometric vector icons. Do not mix icon families. Treat the logo and favicon as distinct assets; never fabricate brand artwork and claim it is official.
- Photography: genuine or properly licensed home detail photography with clear attribution as needed; no glossy unrealistic houses, empty illustrations, fake human portraits or invented before/after results.
- Interaction: deliberate hover/focus states, subtle short transitions; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Avoid
- Oversized hero gradients, floating 3D blobs, glassmorphism, stock-photo walls, 12-item cards, identical round rectangles, drop shadows everywhere, counters without real data, fabricated testimonials and manufactured urgency.
- Excessively tiny grey text, inaccessible low contrast and hamburger-only desktop navigation.

## Required page templates
1. Homepage: clear value statement, seasonal prompt, featured planner/tool, selected evergreen guides, trust/sourcing note.
2. Guide: descriptive breadcrumb, summary answer, clear steps, tools/materials, safety notes, verified sources, last-reviewed date.
3. Calculator/planner: task-specific input form, transparent assumptions, usable output, print/save mode, explanatory text.
4. Topic hub: summary and carefully curated internal links, not keyword-stuffed category pages.
5. About / editorial policy / contact / privacy: real and transparent details, no invented identities.

## Review before shipping
Inspect at 320, 375, 768, 1024, and 1440px widths. Confirm mobile hierarchy, line lengths, focus, skip link, text resizing, touch affordances, nav overflow, image crop and page rhythm. Reject a page if it looks like an undifferentiated AI landing-page template.
