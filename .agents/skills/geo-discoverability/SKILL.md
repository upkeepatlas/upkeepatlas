# Skill: AI Search / GEO Discoverability

## Definition
GEO means improving the usefulness, verifiability, and discoverability of content for AI-assisted search and question-answering systems. It is not a promise of citations or placements.

## Rules
- Google's official guidance says traditional SEO and people-first content remain the basis for AI Overviews and AI Mode; there is no special required AI schema or `llms.txt` file.
- Keep useful answers and factual explanations available in crawlable, visible HTML, not only inside JavaScript apps, accordion-only content, PDF downloads or inaccessible canvas visualizations.
- Put a concise direct answer near the top, then clarify scope, conditions, relevant exceptions and how the result was derived.
- Publish actual original utility (working calculators, downloadable tools, tested checklists, transparent assumptions) rather than echoing generic summaries.
- Link to high-quality primary sources near the claims they support. Use timestamps and revision notes when the information changes.
- Identify who publishes a page and how it was reviewed. Never invent expert involvement, field tests or real-world photos.
- Use descriptive headings and clean semantic structure for readers first. Avoid unnatural keyword repetition, artificial citation bait, spammy FAQ pages or manufactured third-party mentions.
- Allow relevant bots consistent with publisher policy. Robots directives are controls, not promotion hacks; differentiate training-crawler controls from search indexing.
- Never claim that a particular AI vendor will index, cite or recommend a page.

## Practical page pattern
1. Question and short answer.
2. When it applies and what homeowners should check first.
3. Clear steps or a functional tool, with safety limits.
4. References, assumptions and meaningful next steps.
5. Relevant related guides via editorial internal links.

## Validation
Manual inspection of generated HTML, citation links, consistency of numerical assumptions, true schema/visible content alignment, cross-page consistency; after launch monitor genuine search referral data. SEO and GEO status should be reported separately.
