## Boundaries

Calendar month placement beyond explicitly stated source intervals is editorial planning organization, not a local legal mandate or manufacturer service instruction. Paint calculator math is elementary geometry and uses user-configured product label coverage; the resulting gallon estimate isn't independently product-tested.

Every general article directs readers to their specific appliance manual, local rules and qualified professionals for consequential work. Later publishing should record source checks and corrections, and should not automatically claim expert signoff.
