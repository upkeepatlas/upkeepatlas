## Executive summary

**Technical foundation implemented and tested.** Fourteen static indexable routes with specific meta descriptions/titles, semantic headings, canonical URLs on the verified Pages subdomain, an XML sitemap and robots policy, truthful WebSite and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD where applicable, and internal linking. The build and tests are verified via GitHub Actions. Cloudflare's browser-rendering service returned the production homepage HTML and confirmed production metadata and content.

**GEO foundations implemented, but AI referral outcomes are not measurable yet.** General homeowner explanations and source-linked guides are presented in crawlable HTML. Original utilities include a climate/equipment-aware planner, two printable checklists, and a calculation tool with transparent formula.

**NOT a claim of complete SEO, accessibility, or content certification.** Search Console indexing, AI citations, browser usability, real Core Web Vitals and ad approval have not been verified. Some content would benefit from further editorial professional review before serious scale-up.

## Content safety and trust
Guides are AI-assisted and do not claim field testing, licensed expertise or expert sign-off. Maintenance schedules are explicitly framed as starting suggestions; relevant sources include the U.S. EPA, DOE, ENERGY STAR, and CPSC. High-risk roof, electricity, gas, structural, and extensive mold work is directed to qualified professionals. The paint calculator uses disclosed geometry and user-entered manufacturer coverage, not guarantees.

## SEO/GEO implementation notes
The `PUBLIC_SITE_URL` build variable controls whether production HTML is indexable and has absolute canonical URLs. Without a verified HTTPS origin, builds intentionally emit `noindex,nofollow`, robots disallow and an empty sitemap. For verified production, the Cloudflare Pages variable is set to `https://upkeepatlas.pages.dev`.

SEO readiness here means a verifiable technical starting point, not a guarantee of rankings, AI answers, search impressions or ad earnings. Following Google's current Search Central guidance, no special llms.txt or manipulative AI-specific markup is required.
