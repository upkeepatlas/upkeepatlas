# Upkeep Atlas — Project Brief

## Product
A calm, beautiful home-maintenance planner for first-time U.S. homeowners, with a focus on practical seasonal checklists, reliable guides and honest calculators. Users should be able to complete a useful task within a minute of arriving.

## Business
Primary monetization: display advertising after meaningful traffic and publisher compliance. Do not let ad placement damage usability, accessibility or page experience. Commercial claims should be transparent.

## Experience
A refined digital field guide, not a generic AI dashboard. Quiet architectural visual language, editorial serif headlines, considered spacing, evergreen palette, simple bespoke line icons. Design around helpfulness rather than conversion theatrics.

## Proposed information architecture
- / — homepage, seasonal planning, tools, featured guides
- /maintenance-calendar/ — yearly maintenance overview
- /checklists/ — index of real printable checklists
- /seasonal/ — winter/spring/summer/fall hubs
- /calculators/ — practical tools with visible formulas
- /guides/ — evergreen actionable articles
- /about/ — who publishes and how content is created
- /editorial-policy/ — research and review process
- /contact/ and /privacy/ — real contact/privacy information before launch

## Launch principles
- Launch a small, useful set of working experiences, not dozens of placeholder URLs.
- Use the verified production domain for canonical, sitemap, social sharing and Search Console.
- Assume U.S. readers, but label when guidance differs by region, weather, building type or manufacturer.
- Every content entry needs distinct reader intent, primary citations, safety notes and meaningful added value.
- Strictly separate drafts and published pages; no fabricated authors, tests, expertise or social proof.

## Release stages
1. Skills and governance (this commit).
2. Static Astro architecture, design tokens, core layouts and homepage.
3. One functioning maintenance planner plus a small set of carefully researched guides.
4. Automated build/SEO checks and manual accessibility/content review.
5. Verify Cloudflare Pages Git integration, deployed URLs, Search Console and live metrics.
