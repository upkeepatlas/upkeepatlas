## Observed results and opportunity
- https://casaupkeep.com/maintenance — season-specific lists, priority tags and downloadable planner; includes fixed HVAC replacement suggestions that may not match individual equipment.
- https://www.mrhandyman.com/tips-ideas/around-the-house/checklists/ — seasonal printable checklists and contractor-oriented maintenance tasks; less personalized by climate and systems.
- https://checklistforge.com/checklist/seasonal-home-maintenance-checklist — four seasonal checklists and print export; general lists rather than transparent multi-input selection.

Original contribution: a transparent, deterministic selector for season, self-reported broad conditions and four systems; review-first/plan-next ordering, print output and visible source/safety boundaries. No GPS, forecast, account, fake local timing or fabricated service intervals. No media added; form and text provide value.
