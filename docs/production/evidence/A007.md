# A007 — Monthly home maintenance without an overwhelming checklist

Research date: 2026-11-10. Market: United States, English. Candidate: "monthly home maintenance routine"; variants: monthly home maintenance checklist, ten-minute house check, simple monthly home maintenance, home maintenance reminders. Search volume, difficulty, CPC and rankings: **UNMEASURED**.

## Intent and competitor review

- [HomeAlmanac — monthly home maintenance checklist](https://homealmanac.app/guides/monthly-home-maintenance-checklist/): repeating tasks plus month-by-month calendar; our difference is a limited ten-minute observation budget with explicit skip/not-applicable rules.
- [Kept — home maintenance checklist](https://getkeptapp.com/blog/home-maintenance-checklist/): frequency-oriented checklist and fixed recurring suggestions; our guide defers product intervals to manufacturer instructions.
- [Freddie Mac — Home Maintenance Checklist (PDF)](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/docs/home_maintenance.pdf): broad monthly and seasonal checklist; our guide focuses on small, accessible observations and a three-line follow-up record.

Observed search intent: homeowners want manageable recurring tasks and a way to remember them. Competitor observations are based on accessible search results on 2026-11-10, not audited keyword metrics.
