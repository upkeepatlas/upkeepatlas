## Observed U.S.-English search results

- [ChecklistForge — Summer Home Maintenance Checklist](https://checklistforge.com/checklist/summer-home-maintenance-checklist): cooling, seals and exterior task list, printable framing; fixed seasonal chores rather than exposure-specific prioritization.
- [HomeTend — Summer Home Maintenance Checklist](https://www.hometend.co/checklist/summer): exterior and indoor tasks, printable/app framing; prescriptive intervals and tasks that may not apply to all homes.
- [GrabTheHammer — Summer Home Maintenance Checklist](https://grabthehammer.com/summer-home-maintenance-checklist/): step-by-step linked repair checklist and saved progress; less explicit about separating heat/humidity/storm/dry priorities.
- [Homestery — Summer Maintenance Checklist](https://homestery.com/seasonal/summer): interactive task list with HVAC and outdoor care; fixed seasonal framing.

Observed intent: homeowners want a manageable summer checklist and clear task order. Distinction: original **five-exposure decision matrix**, **three-pass observation sequence**, **act/book/defer triage**, and **printable evidence record**. Avoids unsupported universal frequencies, DIY roof/gas/electrical procedures and fabricated cost or energy savings.
