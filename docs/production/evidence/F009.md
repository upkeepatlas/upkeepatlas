## Original improvement
Retains existing rectangular-room estimator and adds mutually exclusive wall-segment mode, independently computed ceiling and measured trim paint, and quart versus whole-gallon purchase scenarios. No universal coverage rate is asserted: homeowners enter their paint-label rate. No media added; math and visible explanations carry the utility.

Risks: wrong opening measurements, textured or porous surfaces, irregular ceilings, separate products and unavailable can sizes. Safety: low-risk planning tool, not paint stripping or lead-disturbance advice. Existing calculator directory remains the internal entry link. Browser input/print/mobile behavior and exact CI must be verified before publication.
