# F013 — Appliance maintenance records tracker
Research date: 2026-11-08. U.S. English. Primary phrase: appliance maintenance log. Variants: appliance service history tracker; appliance model maintenance record; appliance maintenance schedule; appliance equipment register.
Keyword volume, difficulty, CPC and ranking potential: UNMEASURED.

## Search intent and observed competition
- https://realestateledger.io/templates/appliance-maintenance-record-template — printable profile and service log with purchase and warranty fields; marketing upsell.
- https://kopti.app/tools/appliance-passport/ — one-device print-ready passport, tab-local.
- https://removeandreplace.com/maintenance-reminder-tool/ — recurring schedule from generic appliance categories.
- https://www.printlytool.com/en/templates/appliance-maintenance-log/ — printable generic log.
Observed intent: homeowners want to identify a model, preserve its service history and remember next care. Differentiation: several distinct equipment profiles, model-specific manual location, recorded activity linked to equipment, explicit next dates entered by the user, browser-local save, printable record, JSON backup/restore and erase. No invented universal intervals.

## Claim/source ledger
- CPSC recall lookup: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls (reviewed 2026-11-08). This is a separate lookup, not a feature of this tool.
- DOE appliance energy use: https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/estimating-appliance-and-home-electronic-energy-use (reviewed 2026-11-08). Appliance draw/use varies; no energy-saving claim made here.
- Manufacturer manual for the specific model is the source for any actual next-care interval; no universal dates asserted.

Risk: low for recordkeeping, but appliance repair, gas/electric hazards and warranty/recall certainty excluded. No serial numbers requested. Data is browser-local and may be lost; JSON backups are readable. No third-party media used (none adds value to a data-entry tool). Internal links: calculators directory, general maintenance log, annual calendar.
QA: automated validation, backup and ordering tests included. Browser form, print, restore and mobile checks require independent live/preview exercise before release.
