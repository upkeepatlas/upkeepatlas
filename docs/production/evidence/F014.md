# F014 — Home inventory template research (2026-11-08)
Primary intent: home inventory spreadsheet printable; variants: home inventory worksheet insurance, room-by-room possessions CSV, printable household contents list, private home inventory.
U.S. English search inspection: 2026-11-08. Competing examples: https://www.inventorybyhand.com/template/ (printable insurance fields); https://snagatool.com/templates/home-inventory (downloadable spreadsheet with room totals); https://wherekept.com/home-inventory-checklist (printable PDF). Differentiator: browser-local editable room-by-room register, a permanent blank printable worksheet, spreadsheet CSV with formula-prefix escaping, deletion and transparent totals only for supplied prices. No account or photo upload.
Keyword volume, difficulty, CPC, impressions and ranking: UNMEASURED.
