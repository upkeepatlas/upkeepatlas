# A004 — Spring versus fall HVAC maintenance

Research date: 2026-11-10; U.S. English. Primary keyword hypothesis: **spring vs fall hvac maintenance**. Variants: seasonal HVAC maintenance checklist; spring AC tune-up versus fall furnace tune-up; HVAC pre-season maintenance; heat pump spring fall maintenance. Keyword volume, difficulty, CPC and rankings: **UNMEASURED**.

## Observed search intent and differentiation

- [HVAC Compared, Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Checklist](https://www.hvaccompared.com/articles/seasonal-hvac-maintenance-checklist/) — spring/fall task list and estimated service outcomes; some precise lifespan/cost figures were not independently established and are not repeated here.
- [Heating and Cooling, HVAC Maintenance Checklist](https://heatingandcooling.co/hvac-guides/hvac-maintenance-checklist/) — monthly, seasonal and professional lists, including hands-on DIY suggestions.
- [Killen Mechanical, Spring and Fall HVAC Checklist](https://killenmechanical.com/hvac-maintenance-checklist/) — regional contractor checklist with seasonal tasks.
- [One Hour Heating & Air, Spring HVAC Maintenance Checklist (PDF)](https://www.services.onehourheatandair.com/documents/Revised_OHH_SpringHVACMaintenanceChecklist_645708197.pdf) — printable cooling preparation.

Dominant intent: understand what differs by season, what can be done without a contractor, and when to book service. **Original contribution:** system-type service request matrix plus two-column printable contractor handoff that distinguishes observed symptoms from technician measurements and explicitly excludes hazardous DIY work.

## Primary source claim ledger

- [ENERGY STAR Maintenance Checklist](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome/heating-cooling/maintenance-checklist), retrieved 2026-11-10: recommends pre-season contractor cooling checks in spring and heating checks in fall; lists common thermostat, electrical, condensate and controls checks; cooling coils/refrigerant and fuel-heating combustion-specific checks; monthly filter inspection guidance.
- [U.S. DOE, Energy-Saving Hacks to Beat the Summer Heat](https://www.energy.gov/indianenergy/articles/energy-saving-hacks-beat-summer-heat), retrieved 2026-11-10: filter upkeep and professional checks of cooling components.
